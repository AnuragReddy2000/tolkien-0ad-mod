game and retain their own attribution and license.

## Project-generated derivatives

The model notes above also cover their derived skins and animations where the
note lists them. Generated files do not change the license of their upstream
source material.

Project-authored interface illustrations include the procedural selection
backgrounds and concealment icons generated by `tools/make_circle_icon.py`
and `tools/make_concealed_icon.py`, plus the Mordor blight status icon.
Other custom interface portraits are rendered from models whose sources are
credited above or in adjacent `_CREDITS.txt` files.

## Not included in release archives

`art/textures/skins/skeletal/tolkien/troll_trial3/` contains unused trial
textures and is excluded from source commits and packaged builds. The
unreferenced `art/meshes/skeletal/tolkien/warg.dae` is a preview-only work file
and is excluded from source commits and packaged builds.
