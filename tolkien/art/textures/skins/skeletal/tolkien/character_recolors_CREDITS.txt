The Doriath, Erebor, Lindon, and Orc recolor textures in this directory are
generated derivatives of 0 A.D. public character skins. Source files used by
the build scripts include `skeletal_pers_padded_01_01.png` and
`skeletal_pers_linothorax_01_01.png`.
Source art Copyright (C) 2009 Wildfire Games; CC BY-SA 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
Generated outputs include elf_green_01.png, elf_steel_01.png,
erebor_iron_01.png, erebor_leather_01.png, lindon_sea_green_01.png,
lindon_silver_01.png, orc_leather_01.png, and orc_iron_01.png.
The byte-identical duplicate
`art/textures/skins/skeletal/erebor_leather_01.png` has the same source and
license as `art/textures/skins/skeletal/tolkien/erebor_leather_01.png`.
