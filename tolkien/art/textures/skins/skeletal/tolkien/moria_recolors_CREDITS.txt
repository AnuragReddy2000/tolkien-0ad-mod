Moria dwarf clothing, leather, and metal skins are recolored derivatives of
0 A.D. public character textures. The base palettes use
`skeletal_pers_linothorax_01_01.png` and
`skeletal_pers_padded_01_01.png`; subsequent Moria variants are recolored from
the project-made `moria_cloth_01.png` and `moria_iron_01.png`.

moria_cloth_01.png, moria_cloth_red_01.png, moria_cloth_blue_01.png,
moria_iron_02_steel.png, and moria_gold_01.png.
