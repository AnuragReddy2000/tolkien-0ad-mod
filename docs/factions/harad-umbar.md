# Harad & Umbar

## Identity

Desert and coastal raiders defined by mobility, poison attrition, plunder, ships, and Mûmakil.

## Signature mechanics

**Corsair Plunder** is distinct from ordinary 0 A.D. loot. Ordinary loot is a general reward for eligible kills; Corsair Plunder targets profitable enemy military units, ships, and buildings, awarding only a fraction of their value and extending the concept to structures.

**Scourge of the Desert** gives a modest movement advantage on open, sandy, and arid terrain.

**Poison weapons** create damage over time but are not universal and should be counterable through elimination, armor, support, and movement.

## Mûmakil

Mûmakil use established war-elephant concepts:

- very high health and Crush power;
- large footprint and slow maneuverability;
- high cost and population burden;
- vulnerability to concentrated ranged fire, siege, and anti-large tactics;
- a howdah that can garrison a small number of archers.

Garrisoned archers gain extended sight and modestly increased effective range. The Mûmakil remains a mobile ranged platform and trampling unit, but its crew is exposed to the risk of losing the elephant.

## Weaknesses

- Expensive elite units and elephants.
- Vulnerable when mobility is denied.
- Can be punished in static siege warfare.
- Plunder creates momentum but should not become a full-cost refund or infinite economy.
