# Harad & Umbar

## Identity

Harad & Umbar are desert and coastal raiders built around mobility, plunder, poison attrition, and Mûmakil warfare.

## Core mechanics

### Corsair Plunder
Harad receives a partial reward when destroying valuable enemy armies, ships, siege engines, and military buildings. It is stronger than standard 0 A.D. loot because it extends to building destruction and gives a strategic momentum boost, but it must be capped and not become a full cost refund.

Finalized: only a fraction of target Metal cost on military units/siege/ships/military buildings (Wood for economic/market buildings; workers/farms/houses little/none beyond ordinary loot). Fallback fixed values if cost unexposable: inf 1–3, elite 3–6, siege 8–15, military building 10–25 Wood/Metal, ship 8–20. Active v0.2: 10–20% of value (chat 15–25% shelved as snowball).

### Scourge of the Desert
Harad units receive a movement bonus on desert and open arid terrain. This should be moderate and not a universal all-map speed buff. Finalized: +10% infantry/skirmishers on sand/dirt/open, no stacking. Active v0.2: +7%.

### Poison
Harad poison is an attritional threat, not an auto-win effect. It works best against lightly armored or clustered infantry. Finalized: poison-tipped weapons, 5s DoT (v1 2/s) + reduced initial impact, skirmishers only (not army-wide), setup tool vs light/massed, countered by focus fire/cav/clean-up.

### Horse cavalry
Desert-bred swiftness under the Mûmakil hammer (per `cavalry_recommendations.md`):

- Desert Scout — Phase I light cavalry mapping/harass.
- Haradrim Horse Archers + Southron Lance Cavalry — Phase II/III, lightly armored, very fast flankers and hit-and-run harassment; folded into the poison/skirmish raid game.
- Mûmakil remain the Phase III super-unit (see below).

## Mûmakil

Mûmakil are expensive battlefield monsters with:

- high health and trample power;
- large footprints and poor turning;
- strong siege and formation-breaking capacity;
- vulnerable anti-large pressure;
- howdah archers with increased range and sight.

Finalized (Indian war-elephant base, Crush, high pop/cost, weak focus-fire/siege/flank): carries 3–4 designated ranged inf in howdah firing on the move with +LOS and +10–20% range; damage can wound garrison, death ejects survivors; empty = max trample/anti-structure, loaded = ranged+vision at higher value/risk.

### Siege (per `siege_recommendations.md`)
No massive timber catapults/trebuchets — deep deserts and coastal ports lack the vast timber forests to build them.
Finalized: Mûmakil (War Elephant, Mauryan/Seleucid mechanics base) is the defining siege asset — organic battering ram with reinforced tusk-plows + archer-filled towers, trampling defensive infantry ranks and breaking structures/formations. Corsair Plunder bonus on siege kills sustains momentum.

## Strengths

- desert mobility
- raiding economy
- powerful battlefield pressure
- iconic elephant combat

## Weaknesses

- vulnerable when mobility is denied
- expensive elite units and Mûmakil
- punished by static battle and concentrated anti-large tactics

## Structures, units, and heroes

Harad's structure tree and unit roster support raiding and trade, while the heroes focus on ship support, poison pressure, and Mûmakil leadership. The Great Obsidian Palace should improve elephant utility without giving a stack of global health and speed buffs.

Finalized: roster — Harad Skirmisher (poison), Spear Levy, Desert Scout (Phase I light cav), Haradrim Horse Archers + Southron Lance Cavalry (Phase II/III flankers), Corsair Raiding Ship (hit-and-run + infantry delivery, weak vs true navy), Champion Corsair Boarder (fast shock), Mûmakil (centerpiece). Heroes (local): Black Serpent (+15% nearby poison range), Castamir (-30% Corsair ship cost), Suladân (small nearby inf speed + slight Mûmakil out-of-combat heal). Wonder — Great Obsidian Palace: Mûmakil trained while it stands gain howdah capacity or modest mounted-archer range (global +20% HP/speed shelved).

## Counterplay

Attack the raiding fleet, break the Mûmakil, deny desert mobility, and force a battle in terrain or static defense where Harad's fabled pressure is reduced.
