# Mordor — Detailed Design

## Identity

Mordor is industrial darkness: cheap mass production, a separate Snaga worker caste, reconnaissance, territorial miasma, and attrition through numbers.

## Snaga Slaves

Snaga Slaves are Mordor’s gatherers and builders. Military units do not act as general workers. Snaga are cheap but vulnerable, so protecting the worker economy must remain a meaningful task.

## Mass attrition

Mordor units should generally be cheaper and faster to produce, but individually weaker than comparable elite units. Mordor wins by maintaining numbers, multiple fronts, and pressure. It should lose clean equal-quality engagements and suffer when its economy or momentum is interrupted.

## Blighted Desolation

Mordor-controlled territory emits a corrupting miasma. Enemy workers gathering resources inside it suffer a starting penalty of approximately 15% to gather efficiency. The effect may be strongest near major Mordor structures and weaker toward the edge of territory.

It does not assume that enemy farms were built in Mordor territory. It affects workers and resource gathering in the area, does not destroy nodes, and ends when the resource or territory is reclaimed. Mordor workers are unaffected.

## Crebain and Wargs

Crebain are fast, fragile aerial reconnaissance units with large sight and little or no combat. They may receive a temporary reveal or detection ability such as Murder of Crows.

Wargs provide land-based reconnaissance, pursuit, and worker harassment. They are stronger in combat than Crebain but vulnerable to spears, defensive formations, and towers.

## The All-Seeing Eye

Barad-dûr’s active ability is deliberately powerful:

1. The entire map is revealed for approximately 5 seconds.
2. A large search cone or radius then sweeps the map for approximately 30–45 seconds.
3. Enemy units, structures, and concealed forces in the search area are revealed.
4. The cooldown is approximately 8 minutes, subject to testing.
5. Activation produces a clear warning for all players.
6. Barad-dûr must remain standing.

If a moving cone is too difficult to implement reliably, use a 15-second global reveal with an 8–10-minute cooldown. Balance comes from the long cycle, not from making the wonder weak.

## Heroes

- **Sauron:** major combat and area-damage threat.
- **Gothmog:** Orc mass-army and attrition commander.
- **Khamûl:** reconnaissance and raiding hero; improves Crebain sight, Warg and raider mobility, and pressure against workers or isolated units while providing a localized fear effect.

Saruman is better reserved for Isengard, a Shadow alliance, or a scenario because he is not naturally a subordinate Mordor hero.

## Weaknesses

Mordor struggles with elite direct combat, cavalry and flanking, controlled ranged fire, and worker disruption. Cheap units and strong information should not also receive top-tier quality.
