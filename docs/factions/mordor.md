# Mordor

## Identity

Industrial darkness: cheap mass production, a separate worker caste, reconnaissance, territorial miasma, and attrition.

## Signature mechanics

### Snaga Slaves

Snaga Slaves are the primary gatherers and workers. Military units do not replace them as general gatherers. They should be cheap but vulnerable enough that worker protection remains important.

### Blighted Desolation

Mordor-controlled territory emits a corrupting miasma. Enemy workers gathering resources within it suffer a 15% reduction in gathering efficiency. The effect is strongest near major Mordor structures and does not destroy resource nodes or require enemy farms to have been built in Mordor territory.

The effect ends when territory is reclaimed or the resource is no longer within the miasma. Mordor workers are unaffected.

### Crebain and Wargs

Crebain are fast, fragile aerial scouts with strong sight and limited or no combat power. Wargs are land-based reconnaissance and harassment units effective against workers and isolated targets but vulnerable to spears and defensive positions.

### The All-Seeing Eye

Barad-dûr provides a powerful late-game intelligence ability:

1. Entire map revealed for approximately 5 seconds.
2. A large searching vision cone or radius sweeps the map for approximately 30–45 seconds.
3. Enemy units, structures, and concealed forces in the search area are revealed.
4. Approximately 8 minutes between activations, subject to testing.
5. All players receive a clear activation warning.
6. Barad-dûr must remain standing.

If a moving cone is impractical, the fallback is a 15-second global reveal with an 8–10-minute cooldown.

## Heroes

- **Sauron:** major combat and area-damage hero.
- **Gothmog:** Orc mass-army and attrition commander.
- **Khamûl:** reconnaissance, Warg, Crebain, and raiding commander; improves scouting and pursuit and provides localized fear.

Saruman is better reserved for a future Isengard faction, Shadow-aligned scenario, or alliance system rather than the standard Mordor roster.

## Weaknesses

- Individual units are weaker than comparable elite forces.
- Vulnerable to cavalry, flanking, and controlled ranged combat.
- Worker losses can disrupt the industrial economy.
- Pressure fails if Mordor cannot sustain numbers and momentum.
