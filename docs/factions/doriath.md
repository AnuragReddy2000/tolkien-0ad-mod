# Doriath

## Identity

A hidden woodland ambush and defense faction.

## Strengths

- Stealth and ambush tactics.
- Forest-based combat.
- Strong defensive engagements in friendly territory.
- Territorial concealment and sabotage.

## Signature mechanic

The Girdle of Melian degrades enemy detection and movement inside Doriath territory. Menegroth deepens the effect rather than imposing arbitrary global production penalties.

## Weaknesses

- Less effective in open ground.
- Stealth is conditional and can be defeated by scouting and area control.
- Direct assaults against fortified positions are not its ideal strategy.

## Design notes

Camouflage should depend on terrain, movement, or stance. Doriath should not create an entirely unscoutable map.
