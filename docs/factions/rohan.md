# Rohan

## Identity

Rohan is a cavalry-centered horse-lord faction that depends on open ground, scouting, and charge timing.

## Core mechanics

### Pastures of the Mark
Pastures provide a limited Food trickle. They are thematic and useful, but they are not a replacement for farms or a complete economy.

Finalized: Corrals give modest passive Food (small Phase I, scales with tech/livestock, costs Wood, capped — roster v0.2: 0.4 Food/s each, max 4 per CC) + cavalry production/support. Unlimited/free food shelved; farms/hunting/trade still required. Second-wave faction (deferred — no cavalry roster yet).

### Éored Awakening (Phase II cavalry)
Royal Stables (expensive Phase II Food+Metal, slow train, limited numbers): Phase I — Scout Cavalry + light raiders; Phase II — Rohirrim Horse-Archer + Eorlingas Shock; Phase III — Champion King's Guard + top cav techs. Phase II King's Guard shelved.

### Éored Wedge
The wedge formation improves initial contact power and charge cohesion at the cost of turning and control after the first impact. The charge should feel decisive but temporary.

Finalized (Royal Stables tech): temporary charge bonus on entry, breaks after first impact, long cooldown, no melee repeat. Counters: braced spears, forts, terrain, stagger, ranged, slows. Active v0.2: +10% charge damage, +5% pre-contact move, -20% turn, 12s cooldown (v1 multiply-speed/Crush-3s and stale +15%/-25% shelved).

### Stakes and Field Camps
Rohan’s defensive identity comes from temporary field fortification rather than walls. Stakes punish cavalry and charge lanes. Field Camps support forward staging and limited reinforcement.

Finalized: no stone walls/towers. Stakes — cheap Wood, citizen-built, strong vs charges, low HP vs infantry/fire/siege, no territory, not spammable (Horsebreaker Stakes tech: durability + anti-charge damage). Field Camp — Phase II, citizen-built in neutral/friendly, rally + limited territory + basic cav/inf reinforcement + slow nearby-cav repair + modest garrison, weaker than fortress, 2–3 active max, not a CC. Roster v0.2: stakes 12 crush to crossing cavalry.

### Siege — none by design (per `siege_recommendations.md`)
Nomadic horse-culture with zero engineering tradition — absolutely no mechanical siege engines.
Finalized: rely on axe-wielding infantry shock troops with high structural damage bonuses + Fire-arrow technologies researched at camps to slowly burn structures from range. Counterplay weakness to static defenses/siege is intentional.

## Strengths

- early cavalry power
- strong open-ground speed and harassment
- excellent scouting and raiding
- good pressure at the right timing windows

## Weaknesses

- weak against static defensive formations
- vulnerable to siege and braced spearmen
- expensive replacement and high reliance on mobility

## Structures

- corrals, palisades, watchtowers, field camps, cavalry stables, open-field support buildings.

Finalized tree (kept): Phase I — CC, Thatch House, Storehouse, Corral Farm, Field, Wooden Palisade. Phase II — Barracks (infantry/spears), Royal Stables (core cav production), Market, Blacksmith, Wooden Watchtower. Phase III — Great Hall (Fortress, limited central defense), Temple, Meduseld.

## Units

- scout cavalry; Rohirrim horse-archers; shock cavalry; elite cavalry guard; support units and a specialized cavalry doctrine.

Finalized: Scout Cavalry I (fast recon/harass, weak direct), Horse-Archer II (kite/harass, reduced accuracy on move/back-fire, weak spears/towers/terrain), Eorlingas Shock II (flank/exposed-target charge, weak sustained/braced, expensive replace), King's Guard Champion III (high Metal+Food, long train, big pop, weak mass anti-cav/siege).

Roster mapping (per `cavalry_recommendations.md`): Phase I melee + ranged citizen-soldier cavalry train at the CC and keep exceptional mounted gather/hunt rates (offsetting mount cost); the Horse-Archer line carries Phase II/III skirmish; Éored Knights Champion III delivers the formation-shattering charge. Royal Stables stay the Phase II+ production core; Field Camps reinforce basic cav/inf only.

## Heroes

- Eorl; Helm Hammerhand; Théoden. Helm has the horn ability. The hero roster should support a rapid-moving and tactical cavalry plan.

Finalized (chat trio — Éomer/Háma from preview shelved): Eorl (+15–20% nearby initial charge damage + formation speed; +40% trample shelved), Helm (isolated mode: +25% HP +15% armor + fear resist when no allied military near; +50%/+50% shelved — Horn usable in either mode), Helm's Horn (short AoE panic ~3s interrupting attacks/formations, cav loses charge readiness; heroes/fear-immune/structures resist; 4-min cooldown; hard stun / long fear-lock shelved), Théoden ("Arise, Riders!" — nearby cav +speed +charge +snare resist 15–20s, long cooldown; full immunity + double trample +25% 20s shelved).

## Wonder

The wonder should provide a cavalry-command or reinforcement effect, not an uncontrolled free army. It should be powerful but still contextual and limited.

Finalized — Golden Hall of Meduseld: one-time 8–10 cavalry reinforcement (mixed basic + elite, gradual arrival, needs pop, announced — v1 15 elites shelved) + modest local cavalry-command aura. No stacked global eco + army.

## Counterplay

Break Rohan’s timing, stop its flanks, deny open ground, and force it into a prolonged defensive battle or a spear wall with support.
