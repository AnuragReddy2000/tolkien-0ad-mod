# Lothlórien

## Identity

Lothlórien is a forest-defense faction and an elevated ranged power. It privileges trees, visibility control, layered defense, and magical support.

## Core mechanics

### Mallorn cycle and Telain Platforms
The faction gains strong elevation and platform support from mature tree networks. Platforms should be easy to read and easy to counter with siege or broad-force attacks.

Finalized: Seedlings cost Food + Wood, several growth stages → mature Mallorn / wood node → build Telain on top (tree kept, floor clear). Two-tier system: (1) Basic Telain on ordinary mature trees after "Forest Fortification / Woodland Flet / Galadhrim Timbercraft" tech — slower, lower HP, smaller garrison, narrower vision; (2) Superior Mallorn Telain — faster, tougher, better vision/range/garrison. General: needs fully grown tree, 3–5 garrison, less HP than standard tower, strong LOS, weak vs structures, countered by siege/fire/tree-kill, spacing ~60–75% of tower distance, no overlap/adjacency stacking.

Cloaked Way-post (Phase II forward camp, 2–3 max, not CC/Barracks replacement): forward rally, slow out-of-combat heal, limited garrison, local territory + vision + drop-off, modest concealment (reduced LOS, not invisible), pricier than outpost, destroyable.

### Nenya’s Preservation
Defensive structures and home territory gain a local preservation effect. This should not create a permanent invulnerable wall.

Finalized: starting-territory structures cannot be captured (destroy/disable only) + modest defensive bonus + slight wood-gather bonus (smaller than v1 15% — "slightly faster"). Starting territory only, no global eco boost, siege still answers.

## Strengths

- long-range archers
- strong forest terrain economy
- elevated defense and vision
- local defensive integrity

## Weaknesses

- weaker away from forested positions
- vulnerable to cavalry disruption and siege pressure
- loses effectiveness without prepared height and line of sight

## Structures

- forest watch towers; waystations; platform infrastructure; elevated ranged structures; defensive halls; local magical support.

Finalized tree (kept): Village — CC, House, Storehouse, Farmstead, Field, Mallorn Seedling, Dock. Town — Barracks, Market, Blacksmith, Telain Platform, Cloaked Way-post, Outrider Stable (capped, light cav only). City — Fortress, Temple, Caras Galadhon.

## Units

- long-range archers; forest defenders; ranger-like support; elite archers with good vision and strong woodland movement.

Finalized: Galadhrim Scout (keep — Phase II Border Guard Outrider, light cav archer for skirmish/scout with strict training limits so the faction stays infantry-core, not all-in-one best), Wood-cutter (keep — named standard gatherer), Champion Galadhrim Longbowman (keep but rework — longest range, fires while kiting/backpedalling, lower damage than elite ranged, beatable in melee).

### Siege (per `siege_recommendations.md`)
No heavy structural catapults — same Elven constraint as Lindon; forest-defense + elevation instead of timber engines.
Finalized: precise elegant Elven Bolt-Throwers for counter-siege / high-value sniping; walls answered via Infantry Vanguard Saboteurs or Singing-arrows debuffing structural durability. Telain platforms remain vulnerable to enemy siege/fire (see Mallorn cycle).

## Heroes

- a forest guardian; a support hero with vision or morale effects; a ranger commander with local support.

Finalized (local only): Galadriel (brief local light pulse cutting enemy accuracy/vision — v1 5-min 0%-for-12s global shelved), Celeborn (+15% attack speed in forests, nearby only), Haldir (+25% range to garrisoned archers in towers/Telains only).

## Wonder

The wonder supports forest control, vision, or local magical defense rather than a broad all-unit stat bonus.

Finalized — Caras Galadhon Royal Flet: local recruitment/recovery + sight for nearby archers/structures. Global -25% training (v1) shelved. Active v0.2: platform ranged bonus +10% (was +15%), home-territory structure HP +10%.

## Counterplay

Disrupt Lothlórien's forest economy, attack its elevated platforms, and force the faction into open battle with cavalry or siege support.
