# Gondor & Arnor

## Identity

This is a branching Dúnedain faction with a shared Phase I and a Phase II split between ranger mobility and fortress defense.

## Shared Phase I

A strong Dúnedain base with reliable infantry, early stone support, and standard structures sets the foundation. Finalized: CC, House, Storehouse, Farmstead, Field, Outpost, Stone Wall, Stable (Phase I scouts; branch cavalry from Phase II); Line Spearmen + Squire swordsmen (strong, not elite) + Dúnedain Scout (shared light cav). Second-wave faction — branch mechanic deferred past first slice.

## Arnor path

### Line Unbroken
Friendly territory grants a modest morale or resistance effect. The bonus is local and contextual, not a permanent invulnerability. Finalized: +10–15% damage reduction inside own territory (defensive, fades when pushing out). Active v0.2: +10%.

### Ranger identity
Arnor favors stealth, scouting, raids, and ambushes. Rangers are excellent in skirmish and harassment but fragile in direct open-field combat. Finalized: Ranger Camp (forward camp, +LOS/ambush, weaker than barracks); Dúnedain Ranger (high-damage skirmisher, stationary-only stealth, extended LOS, fragile melee).

## Gondor path

### Citadel Legacy
Gondor emphasizes territory projection, towers, and stone structures. Watchtowers and walls support strategic space control without making Gondor unstoppable. Finalized: stronger territory influence + higher wall/tower HP (modest — active v0.2).

### Wall-Mounted Catapults
A proposed Civic Centre upgrade for the Gondor path equips the Civic Centre with wall-mounted catapults. They provide fixed, short-range siege defense and have less range than regular catapults; they are not a replacement for Gondor's field siege engines. The upgrade is available only after choosing the Gondor branch, not the Arnor path, and can be active on only one Civic Centre per player at a time. Research phase, cost, firing strength, and other balance values remain to be defined.

### Citadel Guard defense
The Gondor branch gains strong defensive infantry for chokepoints and static holds. They are slow, paid for, and not ideal in open ground. Finalized: Stonewright Guild + Citadel Stonework (tower HP + repair), Extended Watchtower, Shield-Maiden (ultra-high-defense staller, chokepoint-only).

## Cavalry

Shared Dúnedain base with a branch-divergent top end (per `cavalry_recommendations.md`):
- Shared Phase I — Dúnedain Scout (light cavalry sword/spear): mapping and early gathering; trains at CC/Stable.
- Gondor Phase III — Knights of Dol Amroth (champion heavy spear/lance shock, companion-grade) + Swan Knights (elite heavy cavalry granting nearby infantry a defensive armor aura; hero attachment deferred).
- Arnor Phase II — Arnorian Border Outrider (medium cavalry skirmisher). Arnor Phase III — Dúnedain Rangers on Horseback (versatile cavalry archers: mobility, hit-and-run, high accuracy; no heavy shock).

## Siege (per `siege_recommendations.md`)

Classical standardized doctrine, split by branch — heirs to Númenórean military tradition.
Finalized: Gondor path — heavy static defense, elite Trebuchets / Stoneslings outranging enemy artillery from behind walls (pairs with Citadel Legacy tower/wall bonuses). Arnor path — lighter faster-deploying Mobile Catapults for scattered Angmar threats + deployable Pavise Shields protecting foot archers during sieges.

## Strengths

- strong early infantry and structure quality
- branch-specific identity and tactical choice
- excellent late-game direction when the branch is chosen properly

## Weaknesses

Arnor can be overrun if forced into decisive melee. Gondor is slower and more dependent on prepared ground.

## Heroes and wonder

Aragorn is a shared early hero. Arnor heroes focus on rangers, mobility, and control. Gondor heroes focus on command, defense, and morale. The wonders are subtle but distinct: one favors ranger support, the other defensive structure support. Finalized: Shared I — Aragorn Strider (ignores rough-terrain penalty for accompanying infantry). Arnor III — Aragorn Chieftain, Halbarad (nearby rangers harder to detect), Malbeth (narrowed projectile evasion). Gondor III — Boromir (Horn stuns nearby inf ~3s), Faramir (+25% nearby vs cav/Mûmakil), Denethor (+35% tower fire + cheap repairs), all localized. Wonders: Amon Sûl (Arnor fear immunity) vs White Tower of Ecthelion (Gondor morale + regional tower fire) — local/branch-distinct.

## Counterplay

Arnor is vulnerable to wide scouting and direct engagement; Gondor is vulnerable to ranged pressure, mobility, and siege that bypasses walls and towers.
