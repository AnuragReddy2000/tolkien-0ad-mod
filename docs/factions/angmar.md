# Angmar

## Identity

A dark northern realm of cold, fear, sorcery, and temporary necromantic reinforcement.

## Signature mechanics

**Iron Crown Frost** temporarily reduces the attack speed of enemies engaging Angmar units or structures in close melee.

**Barrow Plague** applies once when an eligible enemy military unit dies within the aura of an Angmar Temple, Dark Temple, or Witch-Fortress:

- normal military unit: approximately 15% conversion chance;
- champions: reduced chance, around 5–10%;
- siege: reduced chance or excluded during initial testing;
- heroes, workers, summoned units, and Angmar units: not eligible.

The chance is a percentage of eligible deaths, not a guaranteed threshold. No separate hard cap is required: the natural limit comes from the invading force, conversion probability, and Thrall lifetime.

## Undead Thralls

Thralls are temporary, weak, controllable melee auxiliaries:

- approximately 40–50% of ordinary infantry health;
- approximately 50–65% of ordinary infantry attack;
- low armor;
- slightly reduced speed;
- no resource or population cost;
- approximately 45-second lifetime with decay;
- cannot gather, construct, repair, garrison, capture, or create additional Thralls;
- can move, attack, patrol, guard, and attack-move.

Each death can trigger at most one conversion, and Thralls cannot trigger further conversions.

## Wonder

The Citadel of Carn Dûm creates a powerful local frost shroud that reduces enemy visibility and firing range near the base. It should remain local rather than becoming a map-wide suppression effect.

## Weaknesses

- Less mobile than cavalry factions.
- Vulnerable to ranged pressure and economy disruption.
- Needs controlled battlefields and structure auras to maximize necromantic value.
