# Erebor

## Identity

Erebor is a wealthy, metal-rich faction that converts hoarded wealth into ranged pressure and industrial strength.

## Core mechanics

### Hoard of the Mountain
Metal wealth improves certain ranged and siege abilities. The bonus is moderate and capped. It should never become a universal global stat multiplier.

Finalized: currently stored Metal (not lifetime) — +1.5% per 1,000 Metal to crossbowmen + siege only, capped +15% (v1 +2%/1k cap +20% shelved). Active v0.2: cap +10% late-game ranged/siege performance. Raiding/forcing spend weakens it. Opening reserve: +100–150 starting Metal (~10–15% of start) counting toward Hoard; spend-vs-keep choice. Physical treasure cache, free caravan, faster early gather, permanent gather bonus — all shelved.

### Glaurung's Legacy
Blacksmith tech, kept: crossbow bolts ignore 25% of target armor (crossbowmen/ranged systems only, not global).

### Cavalry (none by design)
The Lonely Mountain has no cavalry tradition — dense phalanxes, armor, and engineering instead (per `cavalry_recommendations.md`).

Finalized alternatives:
- Dwarven Vanguard Scout — high-armor foot scout that survives map predators; throwing axe with Crippling Shot heavily slowing fleeing game.
- Dwarven Pack Pony — durable non-combat mobile drop-site for early food gatherers (proposed animal, stats pending).

### Trade and metallurgy
Erebor invests heavily in trade, metal output, and specialist production. The faction should feel rich but not unassailable.

Finalized: Trade Caravan (+10% bonus Metal on routes in v1 — keep as vulnerable wealth engine).

### Stone-built structures
Initial balance proposal: for ordinary buildings whose construction is wood-heavy and lore-appropriate as stonework, replace half the Wood cost with Stone while keeping the combined resource cost unchanged. For example, a 150-Wood House becomes 75 Wood + 75 Stone, and a 100-Wood Storehouse becomes 50 Wood + 50 Stone. Leave wood-intensive workshops and siege infrastructure alone, and avoid adding Stone to buildings that already have a substantial Stone cost.

Do not add a dedicated Stone-mining technology yet. Playtest the building-cost shift first; if Stone becomes a sustained bottleneck, consider a modest mining-rate technology and tune it against Erebor's existing Stone income.

### Siege (per `siege_recommendations.md`)
Engineering powerhouse — full standard siege tree with Dwarven durability emphasis.
Finalized: most durable, heavily armored Battering Rams in the game; primary artillery is unique Iron-Head Bolt Thrower (Heavy Ballista) firing massive bolts piercing multiple infantry ranks in a line; Heavy Catapult kept as expensive, vulnerable-unescorted option. Hoard of the Mountain siege bonus applies.

## Strengths

- excellent metallurgy and arsenals
- strong ranged pressure and siege
- economic depth and wealth
- powerful late-game production

## Weaknesses

- expensive units and complex routines
- less effective in open-field melee without support
- vulnerable to economic interruption or raids

## Structures

- metalworks; trade centers; stockpiles; treasure vaults; fortified production structures; ranged-support structures.

Finalized tree (kept): Village — CC, House, Storehouse, Farmstead, Field, Outpost, Stone Wall. Town — Barracks, Market, Blacksmith, Defense Tower. City — Great Keep (Fortress), Temple, Chamber of Thrór.

## Units

- crossbows; artillery; strong ranged infantry; heavy siege units; elite melee support.

Finalized: Mattock-man (worker), Short-swordsman (basic), Champion Iron Crossbowman (key elite — strong not dominant), Iron-Head Bolt Thrower + armored Battering Ram (core siege) + Heavy Catapult (expensive, vulnerable unescorted). Dwarven Vanguard Scout (armored foot scout, Crippling Shot vs game); Dwarven Pack Pony (mobile drop-site, proposed). No Stables, no mounted units.

## Heroes

- a high-craft or wealth-focused commander; a running war engineer; a hero with resource or siege support.

Finalized (localized): Thorin (+15% move to Mattock/worker group in radius), Thrór (-20% building upgrade/wall costs, gated not permanent), Dáin (+20% Hack vs Orcs/Trolls only).

## Wonder

The wonder should improve industrial output, local siege support, or a specific high-value strategic bonus instead of stacking broad combat buffs.

Finalized — Chamber of Thrór: slow steady Metal + Stone trickle into bank (sustain, not snowball burst).

## Counterplay

Disrupt trade routes, attack the economy, and deny Erebor the time to convert wealth into stronger ranged support.
