# Erebor

## Identity

A wealthy Dwarven faction focused on trade, metallurgy, ranged pressure, and siege.

## Strengths

- Strong trade and economic scaling.
- Metal-focused production.
- Crossbows and ranged siege.
- Powerful industrial infrastructure.

## Signature mechanic

Hoard of the Mountain converts accumulated metal wealth into improved ranged and siege effectiveness, with a clear cap to prevent runaway global scaling.

## Weaknesses

- Expensive military.
- Less mobile than cavalry factions.
- Requires time and trade infrastructure to reach full strength.

## Design notes

A modest starting metal reserve may represent inherited wealth, but the economy must still require active mining, trade, and protection.
