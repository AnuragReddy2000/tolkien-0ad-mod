## Balance quirks

- Iron Crown Frost: enemies attacking Angmar structures or units in melee take -10% attack speed for 3s.
- Barrow Plague: enemy deaths in Angmar aura have 15% conversion chance for normal military units; lower for champions or siege; heroes and workers are ineligible.
- Thralls: 45s lifespan, no resource cost, cannot build, gather, or garrison.
- Angmar has strong local battlefield control but should feel weaker in broad open-field equal armies.
- The faction is strongest when it can deploy around temples and fortresses and rotate support through the undead mechanic.
