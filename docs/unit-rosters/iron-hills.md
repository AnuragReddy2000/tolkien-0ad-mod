## Balance quirks

- Iron Wall formation: -20% ranged damage received, +10% resistance to charge disruption, but -20% movement and -15% turn rate while actively braced.
- Repair cost bonus is only active when the unit remains in formation or is within friendly repair support range.
- Iron Hills units do not need to be statically invincible; they are strongest when they control the front and delay movement.
