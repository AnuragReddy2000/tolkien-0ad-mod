## Balance quirks

- Hoard of the Mountain: metal wealth increases siege and ranged damage by up to 10–15% in the late game, but the bonus is capped and tied to stock or bullion thresholds.
- Erebor should be richer than other factions but not automatically stronger in early melee.
- Ballista and siege support are powerful but vulnerable to direct rushes and anti-siege tactics.
