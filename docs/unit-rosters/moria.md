# Moria Unit Roster

## Core philosophy

Moria fights as a durable fortress faction. It wins by controlling chokepoints, preserving its troops, and making enemies pay for entering its underground or fortified terrain.

## Unit roster

### 1. Moria Laborer
- Role: worker
- Cost: low
- HP: low
- Special: deep-mining or tunnel support

### 2. Moria Guardsman
- Role: basic heavy infantry
- Cost: moderate
- HP: medium-high
- Speed: standard
- Special: strong melee and large armor profile

### 3. Moria Spearman
- Role: anti-cavalry infantry
- Cost: moderate
- HP: medium-high
- Special: strong in chokepoints and defensive positions

### 4. Moria Archer
- Role: ranged support
- Cost: moderate
- HP: medium
- Range: ranged attack
- Special: reinforcing the defensive line

### 5. Moria Breaker
- Role: heavy melee bruiser
- Cost: high
- HP: high
- Special: slow but very durable
- Counterplay: siege, ranged fire, and mobility

### 6. Tunnel Runner / Tunnel Scout
- Role: fast underground scout
- Cost: moderate
- HP: moderate
- Special: tunnel movement or underground route control

### 7. Moria Siege Engineer
- Role: siege or heavy support
- Cost: high
- Special: field fortification, siege preparation, or repair support

### 8. Mithril Vanguard
- Role: elite heavy infantry
- Cost: very high
- HP: very high
- Special: heavy melee specialist with excellent durability
- Counterplay: anti-large, cavalry, and broad-range pressure

## Design notes

Moria is strongest in static, planned battles. It should not be a mobile skirmish army. Tunnel network control and chokepoints are its identity.
