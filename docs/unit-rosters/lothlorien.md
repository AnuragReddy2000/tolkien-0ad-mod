## Balance quirks

- Telain Platforms: +15% vision and +10% ranged attack accuracy while on elevated woodland positions.
- Nenya’s Preservation: local structure HP +10% in the faction’s home territory.
- Lothlórien units are strongest when on high ground or in mature forest and weaker in flat open terrain.
- Premium range should be offset by lower melee durability than fully defensive factions.
