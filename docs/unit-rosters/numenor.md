## Balance quirks

- Great Houses: each house grants +5% housing capacity, up to +15% total; not a global double-pop or infinite housing effect.
- Fortified docks receive +15% health and a small local defense bonus when adjacent to a port.
- Númenor does not require all units to be overpowered; it should be defined by consistency, reliability, and elite infrastructure.
