## Balance quirks

- Corsair Plunder: 15–25% of target value for military buildings and elite units; material value is awarded only after destruction or capture; no full refund.
- Scourge of the Desert: +10% speed on desert and arid terrain; not a universal terrain buff.
- Poison deals low damage-over-time and should be countered by moving away, archers, or front-lining units.
- Mûmakil howdah archers gain +10–20% range while inside the howdah and do not grant the elephant full ranged capability.
- Harad is strongest when it maintains mobility and raids from open space; it should be punished when stalled in a static siege or trapped in one lane.
