# Current active balance targets (v0.2)

This file tracks the active version of design balance values after the v0.2 balance pass.

## Shared baseline
- Standard infantry HP target: 90–110
- Standard archer HP target: 55–75
- Standard cavalry HP target: 120–150
- Elite infantry HP target: 150–190
- Elite cavalry HP target: 190–250
- Informal cost ceiling target: elite melee should be at least 1.5x the cost of a standard comparable unit

## Adjusted targets

### Rohan
- Éored Wedge charge damage: +10% (not +15%)
- Turn-rate penalty: -20%
- Helm’s Horn: short disruption only

### Lothlórien
- Platform ranged bonus: +10%
- Home-territory structure HP bonus: +10%

### Erebor
- Hoard bonus cap: +10% late-game ranged/siege performance
- Ballista and siege support remain slow and expensive

### Iron Hills
- Iron Wall ranged-damage reduction: -15%
- Movement reduction: -15%
- Repair support remains a utility bonus, not a reset mechanic

### Númenor
- Housing bonus cap: +10%
- Health premium cap: +5%

### Harad & Umbar
- Corsair Plunder reward: 10–20% of value
- Desert speed bonus: +7%
- Poison remains attritional and tactical

### Angmar
- Frost slow: -8% attack speed for 3s
- Barrow Plague: ~15% conversion chance
- Thrall lifetime: ~45s

### Mordor
- Blighted Desolation: ~15% gather-rate reduction
- Eye reveal: long cooldown, limited sweep

### Lindon
- Gift of the West: naval, defensive, and high-craft techs only
- Swan Ship capacity limit: small infantry transport or resource-supported reinforcement

### Doriath
- Stealth requires terrain and movement conditions
- Open-field ambush value remains lower than elite Dúnedain or Mordor units

### Moria
- Mithril Core: 50% yield while structure is active
- Tunnel movement bonus: +7%

### Gondor & Arnor
- Arnor Line Unbroken: +10% resistance in friendly territory
- Gondor walls and towers remain modestly stronger, not an auto-win defense layer
